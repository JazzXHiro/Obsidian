#### **Q.** Simple Interest on 5000 at 10 percent for 2 years is:

- 900
    
- 1000
    
- 1100
    
- 1200

**Sol-**

Let’s calculate it cleanly.

**Simple Interest formula:**  
 
$SI = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$

Given:

- Principal (P = 5000)
    
- Rate (R = 10%)
    
- Time (T = 2) years

$SI = \frac{5000 \times 10 \times 2}{100} = 1000$

✅ **Correct answer:** **1000**

#### **Q.** The perimeter of a square is 40 cm. Find its area.

- 100
    
- 121
    
- 144
    
- 160

**Sol** - Let’s calculate it precisely.

**Perimeter of a square** (= 4 \times \text{side})

Given:  

$$4 \times \text{side} = 40  
Rightarrow \text{side} = 10 \text{ cm}$$

**Area of a square**:  
 
$text{Area} = \text{side}^2 = 10^2 = 100$

✅ **Correct answer:** **100**
