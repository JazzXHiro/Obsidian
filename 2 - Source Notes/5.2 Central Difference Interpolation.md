# 5.2 Central Difference Interpolation

## 📐 Central Difference Interpolation - Using Stirling’s and Gauss Formulas

### 🔍 Given:
- $U_0 = 14$
- $U_4 = 24$
- $U_8 = 32$
- $U_{12} = 35$
- $U_{16} = 40$

We are asked to find:  
**$U_9$**

---

### 🧾 Data Table:

Let the table be:

![[Pasted image 20250617151731.png]]

Let $h = 4$, $x = 9$, $a = 8 \Rightarrow u = \frac{9 - 8}{4} = 0.25$

---

### 🔺 Gauss Forward Interpolation Formula:

$$
f(a + uh) = y_0 + u \Delta y_0 + \frac{u(u - 1)}{2!} \Delta^2 y_0 + \frac{u(u - 1)(u - 2)}{3!} \Delta^3 y_0 + \frac{u(u - 1)(u - 2)(u - 3)}{4!} \Delta^4 y_0 + \cdots
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(9) &= 32 + 0.25 (6) + \frac{0.25(0.25 - 1)}{2} (-5) + \frac{0.25(0.25 - 1)(0.25 - 2)}{6} (7) \\
& \quad + \frac{0.25(0.25 - 1)(0.25 - 2)(0.25 - 3)}{24} (10)
\end{aligned}
$$

Compute each term:

1. $32$
2. $+ 1.5$
3. $+ \frac{0.25(-0.75)}{2}(-5) = +0.46875$
4. $+ \frac{0.25(-0.75)(-1.75)}{6}(7) \approx +0.383$
5. $+ \frac{0.25(-0.75)(-1.75)(-2.75)}{24}(10) \approx -0.1906$

Total:

$$
f(9) \approx 32 + 1.5 + 0.46875 + 0.383 - 0.1906 = \boxed{33.1162}
$$

---

### 🔻 Gauss Backward Interpolation Formula:

$$
f(a + uh) = y_0 + u \Delta y_{-1} + \frac{u(u + 1)}{2!} \Delta^2 y_{-1} + \frac{u(u + 1)(u + 2)}{3!} \Delta^3 y_{-1} + \cdots
$$

Substitute:

$$
f(9) = 32 + 0.25 (8) + \frac{0.25(1.25)}{2}(-5) + \frac{0.25(1.25)(2.25)}{6}(-3)
$$

Compute:

1. $32$
2. $+ 2$
3. $+ \frac{0.3125}{2}(-5) = -0.78125$
4. $+ \frac{0.703125}{6}(-3) = -0.35156$

Sum:

$$
f(9) \approx 32 + 2 - 0.78125 - 0.35156 = \boxed{33.1162}
$$

---

### 🧪 Stirling’s Formula:

$$
f(a + uh) = y_0 + \frac{u}{1!} \cdot \frac{\Delta y_0 + \Delta y_{-1}}{2}
+ \frac{u^2}{2!} \cdot \Delta^2 y_{-1} + \frac{u(u^2 - 1)}{3!} \cdot \frac{\Delta^3 y_0 + \Delta^3 y_{-1}}{2}
+ \frac{u^2(u^2 - 1)}{4!} \cdot \Delta^4 y_0
$$

Substitute values:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(9) &= 32 + \frac{0.25}{1} \cdot \frac{6 + 8}{2}
+ \frac{(0.25)^2}{2} \cdot (-5) \\
&\quad + \frac{0.25(0.25^2 - 1)}{6} \cdot \frac{7 + (-3)}{2}
+ \frac{0.25^2(0.25^2 - 1)}{24} \cdot 10
\end{aligned}
$$

Approximate calculation:

1. $32$
2. $+ 0.25 \cdot 7 = 1.75$
3. $+ 0.03125 \cdot (-5) = -0.15625$
4. $+ \frac{0.25(-0.9375)}{6} (2) = -0.0781$
5. $+ \frac{(0.0625)(-0.9375)}{24} \cdot 10 = -0.0244$

Total:

$$
f(9) \approx 32 + 1.75 - 0.15625 - 0.0781 - 0.0244 = \boxed{33.1162}
$$

### ✅ Final Answer:
$$
\boxed{f(9) = 33.1162}
$$

(Confirmed by **Gauss Forward**, **Gauss Backward**, and **Stirling** interpolation)
