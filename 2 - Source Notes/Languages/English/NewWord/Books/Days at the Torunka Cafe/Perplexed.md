# Perplexed

**Meaning:**
Feeling confused, puzzled, or unsure about something because it’s hard to understand.

👉 **In simple words:** your mind is stuck and you don’t know what to think.

**Example sentences:**
She looked perplexed by the sudden change in plans.
I was perplexed by the math problem.
His expression was perplexed, as if he had no idea what was going on.

**Pronunciation:**
per-PLEKST
Phonetic: /pəˈplekst/
(Stress is on PLEKST)

**Synonyms:**
Confused
Puzzled
Baffled
