# Dzięki

The Polish word **„Dzięki”** (meaning “thanks”) is pronounced:

### **/ˈdʑɛŋ.kʲi/**

Or more simply, it sounds like:

### **“JENK-ee”**

(with a soft "J" at the start, like in "jeans," and a slightly nasal "EN")

---

### Breakdown:

- **„Dź”** → sounds like the English **"j"** in **"juice,"** but a bit softer.
- **„ę”** → a nasalized vowel, like **"en"** in French “en,” but here it often sounds closer to **"en"** or **"em"** depending on region and speed.
- **„k”** → like **"k"** in **"kite."**
- **„i”** → like **"ee"** in **"see."**

### So:

**Dzięki** ≈ “**JENK-ee**” (with a soft "j" and slight nasal on "en")
