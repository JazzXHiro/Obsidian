# Dobzre

It looks like you're referring to the Polish word „dobrze”, which means “well” or “okay” (used to say "I'm fine" or "Alright").

✅ Correct spelling: dobrze

Pronunciation:

IPA: /ˈdɔb.ʐɛ/

Breakdown:

do → like daw in daughter

brz → makes a sound like the "zh" in measure (a voiced "sh")

e → like e in bed


Approximate English pronunciation:

“DOHB-zheh”
(soft “zh” sound like in treasure)
