# Dzień Dobry

Great choice! The Polish phrase „Dzień dobry” means “Good day” or “Hello” — it’s a polite and common greeting used throughout the day.


---

✅ Correct pronunciation:

IPA: /d͡ʑɛɲ ˈdɔ.brɨ/


---

Breakdown:

Dzień = day

dź → like a soft "j" in jeans, but more palatal (tongue high)

eń → like "en" in onion, but with a soft "ny" sound at the end


✅ Approximate: "jen" (with a soft ny at the end — like jyen)

Dobry = good

do → like daw in daughter

bry → "brih" with the y like the i in myth (not "bree")


✅ Approximate: "DOH-brih"


---

Full phrase pronunciation:

“Jyen DOH-brih”
(soft “j” + soft “ny” + clear and polite tone)
