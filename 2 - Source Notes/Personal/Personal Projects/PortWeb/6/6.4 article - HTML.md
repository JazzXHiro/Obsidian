# article - HTML

`<article>` is a **semantic HTML element** used to represent a **self-contained, independent piece of content**.

---

## What `<article>` means

An `<article>` is content that:

- **Makes sense on its own**
    
- Could be **reused, shared, or syndicated**
    
- Does not depend on surrounding content to be understood
    

Think: _“If I copy-paste this somewhere else, does it still make sense?”_  
If yes → `<article>` is appropriate.

---

## Typical uses of `<article>`

- Blog posts
    
- News stories
    
- Portfolio project cards
    
- Forum posts
    
- Product listings
    
- Comments

Example:

```html
<article class="project">
  <h3>Resume Website</h3>
  <p>An interactive portfolio built with HTML, CSS, and JS.</p>
</article>
```

This project card stands on its own → correct use of `<article>`.

---

## `<article>` vs `<section>` (key distinction)

|Element|Purpose|
|---|---|
|`article`|Independent, reusable content|
|`section`|Thematic grouping within a page|

### Example together (correct pattern)

```html
<section id="projects">
  <h2>Projects</h2>

  <article class="project">Project A</article>
  <article class="project">Project B</article>
</section>
```

- `section` = topic (“Projects”)
    
- each `article` = standalone item
    

---

## `<article>` vs `<div>`

|Element|Semantic meaning|
|---|---|
|`article`|Yes (content-focused)|
|`div`|No (layout-only)|

Use `<div>` only when **no meaning applies**.

---

## Accessibility & SEO impact

- Screen readers treat `<article>` as a **landmark**
    
- Search engines can index articles as **individual content units**
    
- Improves document structure and clarity

---

## Rules of thumb (practical)

Use `<article>` when:

- The content could have its own heading
    
- The content could appear in RSS, search results, or embeds
    
- The content is one “item” among many
    

Do **not** use `<article>`:

- For layout wrappers
    
- For containers that only exist for styling

---

## Interview-ready explanation

> “`<article>` represents a self-contained, independent piece of content that can stand on its own, such as a blog post or portfolio item.”

---

## One-line rule

**If the content is reusable and independent, use `<article>`.**
