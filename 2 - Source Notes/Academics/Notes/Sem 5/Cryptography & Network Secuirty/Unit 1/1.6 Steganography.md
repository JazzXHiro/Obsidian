# Steganography

**Steganography** is an alternative to encryption. Instead of transforming the message to be unreadable, steganography focuses on **hiding the existence of the message** itself.

• **Methods:** Hiding includes using invisible ink, using only a subset of letters/words within a longer cover message, or hiding information in the Least Significant Bit (LSB) of a graphic image or sound file.

• **Drawback:** Steganography has a high overhead required to hide a relatively small amount of information.
