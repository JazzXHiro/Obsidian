# Deterministic Finite Automata

## Automaton & Its Configuration

### 1. Automaton as Recognizer or Acceptor
- Automaton takes an **input string** $w$.
- It checks whether $w \in L$ over $\Sigma$.

Results:
- **YES** → $w$ is a valid string.
- **NO** → $w$ is an invalid string.

---

### 2. Automaton as Generator, Enumerator, or Transducer
- Automaton takes an **input string** $w$.
- Automaton produces **output**.

Result:
- **Output string** $w$

---

## Finite Automata

- Finite automata are mainly used for **pattern recognition**.  

- It takes the **string of symbols** as **input** and changes its **state** accordingly.  

- When the desired symbol is found, then a **transition** occurs.  

- At the time of transition, the automaton can either move to the **next state** or stay in the **same state**.  

- Finite automata have two states:  
  - **Accept state**  
  - **Reject state**
