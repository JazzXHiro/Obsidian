# Statistics

## What is statistics?

Statistics is the science of:
- Collecting data
- Organizing data
- Analyzing data
- Interpreting results for decision making

In software systems, we rarely have complete data. Instead of perfect information, we work with partial logs, sample user data, noisy sensor readings etc. Statistics allows us to make reliable decisions despite uncertainity.

## Descriptive vs Inferential Statistics

### Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics answers:
- What is happening in the system?
- What does the data look like?

Common tools:
- Mean, median, mode
- Variance and standard deviation
- Graphs and visual summaries

### Inferential Statistics

Inferential statistics answers:
- Why is this happening?
- Will this pattern continue?

It allows us to generalize samples to larger systems.

## Statistics vs Data Analysis vs Data Science

- **Statistics:** Mathematical reasoning behind uncertainty

- **Data Analysis:** Cleaning, transforming, visualizing logs and metrics.

- **Data Science:** Prediction, personalization, AI-driven behavior.

Statistics validates whether your analytics or ML results are trustworthy.
