# Product and Process Quality Standards

## ✅ Product Quality Standards

### 🔹 Definition

Standards that define **attributes and criteria** a software product must meet to be considered of high quality.

### 🔹 Key Product Quality Attributes (as per ISO/IEC 25010):

1. **Functionality** – Does the product meet specified requirements?
    
2. **Reliability** – How well does it perform under stated conditions for a specified time?
    
3. **Usability** – Is the software easy to understand, learn, and operate?
    
4. **Efficiency** – Uses resources (CPU, memory) effectively.
    
5. **Maintainability** – Ease of correcting faults or making improvements.
    
6. **Portability** – Ability to run in different environments or platforms.
    

### 🔹 Common Product Quality Standards:

- **ISO/IEC 25010** – International standard for system and software quality models.
    
- **IEEE 730** – Standard for software quality assurance plans.
    
- **ISO 9126** _(superseded by ISO 25010)_ – Defined software quality characteristics.
    

---

## ✅ Process Quality Standards

### 🔹 Definition

Standards that govern **how** software is developed, tested, and maintained. Focus is on **methodology and practices** used in software engineering.

### 🔹 Objectives

- Ensure consistency and repeatability
    
- Improve productivity and product quality
    
- Facilitate continuous process improvement
    

### 🔹 Popular Process Standards:

1. **ISO 9001**
    
    - Generic quality management system (QMS) standard.
        
    - Ensures documented processes, regular audits, and customer satisfaction.
        
2. **CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration)**
    
    - Maturity model with levels 1–5:
        
        - Level 1: Initial
            
        - Level 2: Managed
            
        - Level 3: Defined
            
        - Level 4: Quantitatively Managed
            
        - Level 5: Optimizing
            
    - Focuses on improving process maturity.
        
3. **Six Sigma**
    
    - A data-driven approach to eliminate defects and improve process efficiency.
        
    - Emphasizes statistical control and continuous improvement.
        
4. **SPICE (ISO/IEC 15504)**
    
    - A framework for assessing software development processes.
        
    - Supports process improvement across projects and organizations.
        

---

## 🔁 Product vs Process Quality

|Aspect|Product Quality|Process Quality|
|---|---|---|
|Focus|End result (software product)|Development method and lifecycle|
|Ensured By|Testing, QA metrics|Audits, standards (e.g., ISO 9001)|
|Goal|Meet user expectations|Consistent high-quality development|
|Standards Used|ISO 25010, IEEE 730|ISO 9001, CMMI, SPICE|
