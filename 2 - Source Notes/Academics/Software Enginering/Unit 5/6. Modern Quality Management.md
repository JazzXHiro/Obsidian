# Modern Quality Management

Modern Quality Management (MQM) refers to contemporary practices, tools, and principles that ensure high-quality software while emphasizing customer satisfaction, continuous improvement, and collaborative development.

![[Pasted image 20250515073002.png]]

## Key Principles of Modern Quality Management

1. **Customer-Centric Approach**  
   - Quality = Meeting customer needs/expectations.  
   - Uses **user feedback**, **continuous delivery**, and **fast iterations**.  

2. **Total Quality Management (TQM)**  
   - **Everyone** owns quality.  
   - Focus on **process improvement**, **employee involvement**, and **long-term success**.  

3. **Continuous Improvement (Kaizen)**  
   - Small, **incremental** improvements in processes/code.  
   - Encourages **experimentation**, **learning**, and **feedback loops**.

4. **Risk Management & Prevention**  
   - **Shift-left**: Focus on **defect prevention** (not just detection).  
   - Methods: **Early testing**, **code reviews**, **static analysis**.  

5. **Process Metrics & Automation**  
   - **Automation**: CI/CD pipelines, automated testing.  
   - **Key Metrics**: Code coverage, defect density, deployment frequency.
