# Software Quality Assurance

# Software Quality Assurance (SQA)

- **Definition**: Ensures quality through processes, procedures, and standards implementation.  

### Focus Areas:  
- **Portability**  
- **Usability**  
- **Reusability**  
- **Correctness**  
- **Maintainability**  
- **Error Control**

# Elements of Software Quality Assurance (SQA)

1. **Standards**:  
   Ensure compliance with IEEE, ISO, and other standards.  

2. **Reviews & Audits**:  
   - *Technical Reviews*: Error detection by software engineers for software engineers.  
   - *Audits*: Are a type of interview with SQA personnel (people employed in an organization) ensuring that quality guidelines are being followed for software engineering work.  

3. **Testing**:  
   Plan and execute tests to identify errors. The job of SQA is to ensure that testing is properly planned and efficiently conducted for primary goal of software. 

4. **Error/Defect Collection & Analysis**:  
   SQA collects and analyzes error and defect data to better understand how errors are introduced and what software engineering activities are best suited to eliminating them.

5. **Change Management**:  
   Oversee proper change implementation.  

6. **Education**:  
   Train engineers/managers on best practices. The SQA organization takes the lead in software process improvement which is key proponent and sponsor of educational programs.

7. **Security Management**:  
   Implement processes/tech for software security.  

8. **Safety**:  
   Assess failure impact and mitigate risks.  

9. **Risk Management**:  
   The SQA organization ensures that risk management activities are properly conducted and that risk-related contingency plans have been established.

## What is verification and validation in quality assurance?

Validation is the process of checking whether the specification captures the customer's requirements, while verification is the process of checking that the software meets specifications.

## SQA Plans

## **Quality Assurance Plan (QAP)**  
A document outlining processes, procedures, and standards to ensure product/service quality.  

### **Importance**:  
1. **Quality Standards**: Ensures adherence to guidelines.  
2. **Risk Management**: Identifies and mitigates risks.  
3. **Standardization**: Maintains consistency across processes.  
4. **Customer Satisfaction**: Enhances product reliability.  
5. **Resource Optimization**: Efficient use of time and tools.  
6. **Early Issue Detection**: Reduces cost of late fixes.

### Steps to Develop an SQA Plan

1. **Define Project Objectives & Scope**  
2. **Identify Quality Standards & Criteria**  
3. **Identify Stakeholders**  
4. **Define Roles & Responsibilities**  
5. **Conduct Risk Assessment**  
6. **Establish QA Activities**  
7. **Develop Testing & Inspection Procedures**  
8. **Document Processes & Procedures**  
9. **Establish Documentation and Reporting Mechanisms**
10. **Allocate Resources & Training**  
11. **Define Change Control Processes**  
12. **Review & Approval**  
13. **Monitor & Continuously Improve**  
14. **Communicate & Train Stakeholders:** Communicate the Quality Assurance Plan to all relevant stakeholders and provide training as necessary. Ensure that everyone involved understands their roles and responsibilities in maintaining and improving quality.
