# Software Quality Assurance

# Software Quality Assurance (SQA)

- **Definition**: Ensures quality through processes, procedures, and standards implementation.  

### Focus Areas:  
- **Portability**  
- **Usability**  
- **Reusability**  
- **Correctness**  
- **Maintainability**  
- **Error Control**

# Elements of Software Quality Assurance (SQA)

1. **Standards**:  
   Ensure compliance with IEEE, ISO, and other standards.  

2. **Reviews & Audits**:  
   - *Technical Reviews*: Error detection by software engineers for software engineers.  
   - *Audits*: Are a type of interview with SQA personnel (people employed in an organization) ensuring that quality guidelines are being followed for software engineering work.  

3. **Testing**:  
   Plan and execute tests to identify errors. The job of SQA is to ensure that testing is properly planned and efficiently conducted for primary goal of software. 

4. **Error/Defect Collection & Analysis**:  
   SQA collects and analyzes error and defect data to better understand how errors are introduced and what software engineering activities are best suited to eliminating them.

5. **Change Management**:  
   Oversee proper change implementation.  

6. **Education**:  
   Train engineers/managers on best practices. The SQA organization takes the lead in software process improvement which is key proponent and sponsor of educational programs.

7. **Security Management**:  
   Implement processes/tech for software security.  

8. **Safety**:  
   Assess failure impact and mitigate risks.  

9. **Risk Management**:  
   The SQA organization ensures that risk management activities are properly conducted and that risk-related contingency plans have been established.
