# Software Quality Assurance (SQA)

- **Definition**: Ensures quality through processes, procedures, and standards implementation.  

### Focus Areas:  
- **Portability**  
- **Usability**  
- **Reusability**  
- **Correctness**  
- **Maintainability**  
- **Error Control**

# Elements of Software Quality Assurance (SQA)

1. **Standards**:  
   Ensure compliance with IEEE, ISO, and other standards.  

2. **Reviews & Audits**:  
   - *Technical Reviews*: Error detection by engineers.  
   - *Audits*: Verify adherence to quality guidelines.  

3. **Testing**:  
   Plan and execute tests to identify errors.  

4. **Error/Defect Collection & Analysis**:  
   Track errors to improve processes.  

5. **Change Management**:  
   Oversee proper change implementation.  

6. **Education**:  
   Train engineers/managers on best practices.  

7. **Security Management**:  
   Implement processes/tech for software security.  

8. **Safety**:  
   Assess failure impact and mitigate risks.  

9. **Risk Management**:  
   Ensure risk activities and contingency plans are in place.
