# Important Questions

**Q1. Five application areas of digital image processing**

1. **Medical Imaging**: enhancement (e.g. MRI, CT scans), segmentation for diagnosis and 3D reconstruction.
    
2. **Remote Sensing**: satellite/aerial image analysis for land-use classification, crop monitoring.
    
3. **Biometrics**: fingerprint, iris, and facial recognition for security systems.
    
4. **Industrial Inspection**: defect detection on assembly lines (e.g. surface scratches, weld quality).
    
5. **Entertainment & Multimedia**: image/video compression (JPEG, MPEG), special effects in film.
