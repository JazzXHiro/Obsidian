# 📘 Lagrange’s Interpolation Formula

## 🔍 Problem:
Find the value of $y$ when $x = 10$ using **Lagrange’s Interpolation Formula**.

### 📊 Given Data:

| $x$ | 5 | 6 | 9 | 11 |
|-----|---|---|---|----|
| $f(x)=y$ | 12 | 13 | 14 | 16 |

---

### 📌 Formula:

$$
f(x) = \sum_{i=0}^{n} y_i \prod_{\substack{j=0 \\ j \ne i}}^{n} \frac{x - x_j}{x_i - x_j}
$$

---

### Logical Method instead of Formula

![[5.3 Lagrange Interpolation 2025-06-25 11.42.17.excalidraw]]

---
### 🧮 Applying Formula:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= \frac{(x - 6)(x - 9)(x - 11)}{(5 - 6)(5 - 9)(5 - 11)} \cdot 12 \\
&+ \frac{(x - 5)(x - 9)(x - 11)}{(6 - 5)(6 - 9)(6 - 11)} \cdot 13 \\
&+ \frac{(x - 5)(x - 6)(x - 11)}{(9 - 5)(9 - 6)(9 - 11)} \cdot 14 \\
&+ \frac{(x - 5)(x - 6)(x - 9)}{(11 - 5)(11 - 6)(11 - 9)} \cdot 16
\end{aligned}
$$

---

### 🔢 Substituting $x = 10$:

#### First Term:
$$
\frac{(10 - 6)(10 - 9)(10 - 11)}{(5 - 6)(5 - 9)(5 - 11)} \cdot 12 = \frac{(4)(1)(-1)}{(-1)(-4)(-6)} \cdot 12 = \frac{-4}{24} \cdot 12 = -2
$$

#### Second Term:
$$
\frac{(10 - 5)(10 - 9)(10 - 11)}{(6 - 5)(6 - 9)(6 - 11)} \cdot 13 = \frac{(5)(1)(-1)}{(1)(-3)(-5)} \cdot 13 = \frac{-5}{15} \cdot 13 = -\frac{65}{15} \approx -4.33
$$

(But in screenshot it's directly calculated as: $-\frac{13}{3}$)

#### Third Term:
$$
\frac{(10 - 5)(10 - 6)(10 - 11)}{(9 - 5)(9 - 6)(9 - 11)} \cdot 14 = \frac{(5)(4)(-1)}{(4)(3)(-2)} \cdot 14 = \frac{-20}{-24} \cdot 14 = \frac{5}{6} \cdot 14 = \frac{35}{3}
$$

#### Fourth Term:
$$
\frac{(10 - 5)(10 - 6)(10 - 9)}{(11 - 5)(11 - 6)(11 - 9)} \cdot 16 = \frac{(5)(4)(1)}{(6)(5)(2)} \cdot 16 = \frac{20}{60} \cdot 16 = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 16 = \frac{16}{3}
$$

---

### 🧾 Final Calculation:

$$
f(10) = 4 - \frac{13}{3} + \frac{35}{3} + \frac{16}{3}
$$

Group terms:

$$
f(10) = 4 + \frac{38}{3} = \frac{12 + 38}{3} = \frac{50}{3} = 16.66\overline{6}
$$

---

### ✅ Answer:

**$f(10) = \boxed{16.\overline{6}}$**
