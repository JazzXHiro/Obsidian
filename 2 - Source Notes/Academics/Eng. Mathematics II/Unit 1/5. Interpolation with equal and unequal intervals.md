# 5. Interpolation with equal and unequal intervals

# 📈 Interpolation

## Definition

> The technique or method of estimating unknown values from given set of observation is known as **interpolation**.

---

## Types of Interpolation Based on Interval

### 1. Equal Interval
- **Newton Forward Formula**
- **Newton Backward Formula**
- **Gauss Forward / Gauss Backward**
- **Stirling's Formula**
- **Bessel's Formula**

### 2. Unequal Interval
- **Newton's Divided Difference Formula**
- **Lagrange's Interpolation Formula for Unequal Interval**

---

## Example Data Table(Equal Intervals)

Given:
| $x$     | $f(x)$ |
|---------|--------|
| 1971    | 1000   |
| 1981    | 1025   |
| 1991    | 1080   |
| 2001    | 1120   |
| 2011    | 1200   |

Estimate values for:
- 1973
- 1995
- 2008
