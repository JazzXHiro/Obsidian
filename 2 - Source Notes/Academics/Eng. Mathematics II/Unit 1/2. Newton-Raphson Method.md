# Newton-Raphson Method

## 1.  Idea  
Use the tangent line at a current guess $x_0$ to approximate the root of  
$$
f(x) = 0
$$  
by finding where that tangent crosses the $x$-axis.

---

## 2. Equation of the Tangent at $(x_0, f(x_0))$  
The tangent line to $y = f(x)$ at $x = x_0$ is given by:  
$$
y - f(x_0) \;=\; f'(x_0)\,\bigl(x - x_0\bigr).
$$

Set $y=0$ (we want the $x$-intercept), so  
$$
0 - f(x_0) \;=\; f'(x_0)\,\bigl(x_1 - x_0\bigr)
$$  
which gives  
$$
x_1 - x_0 \;=\; -\,\frac{f(x_0)}{f'(x_0)}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x_1 \;=\; x_0 \;-\;\frac{f(x_0)}{f'(x_0)}.
$$

---

## 3. Iteration Formula  
Repeat the process to refine the root approximation:  
$$
x_{n+1} \;=\; x_n \;-\;\frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}, \; {f'(x_{n})} \neq 0
$$
