# Euler's Method

# 📘 Euler & Euler Modified Method

## Euler Method

Euler's iterative formula:

$$
y_{n+1} = y_n + h f(x_n, y_n)
$$

---

### Problem 1:

Solve $\frac{dy}{dx} = x + y$  
Given: $x_0 = 0$, $y_0 = 1$  
Find approximate value of $y$ at $x = 0.1$

---

### Step size:

$$
h = \frac{0.1 - 0}{5} = 0.02
$$

> [!NOTE] Note
> The higher the step-size more accurate the answer is.

### Steps:

- $x_0 = 0$
- $x_1 = 0.02$
- $x_2 = 0.04$
- $x_3 = 0.06$
- $x_4 = 0.08$
- $x_5 = 0.10$

---

### Calculations:

#### Put $n = 0$:
$$
y_1 = y_0 + h (x_0 + y_0) = 1 + 0.02 (0 + 1) = 1.02
$$

#### Put $n = 1$:
$$
y_2 = y_1 + h (x_1 + y_1) = 1.02 + 0.02 (0.02 + 1.02) = 1.0408
$$

#### Put $n = 2$:
$$
y_3 = y_2 + h (x_2 + y_2) = 1.0408 + 0.02 (0.04 + 1.0408) = 1.0624
$$

#### Put $n = 3$:
$$
y_4 = y_3 + h (x_3 + y_3) = 1.0624 + 0.02 (0.06 + 1.0624) = 1.0847
$$

#### Put $n = 4$:
$$
y_5 = y_4 + h (x_4 + y_4) = 1.0847 + 0.02 (0.08 + 1.0847) = 1.108
$$

---

### Final Approximate Answer:
$$
y(0.1) \approx 1.108
$$
