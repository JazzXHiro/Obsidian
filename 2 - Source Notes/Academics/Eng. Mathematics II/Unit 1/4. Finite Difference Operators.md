# Finite Difference Operators

# Numerical Operator and Their Relation

## $1.$ Forward Difference Operator:

Let $y = f(x)$ be a function where the argument $x$ can take the equidistant values  
$$a,\ a+h,\ a+2h,\ \dots,\ a+nh$$  
and $f(a),\ f(a+h),\ \dots,\ f(a+nh)$ are values of the given points.


> [!NOTE] Note
> h is the step size here.
	



---

### Definition:

The **forward difference operator** is defined as:

$$
\Delta f(x) = f(x + h) - f(x)
$$

---

### Higher Order Differences:

The **second order forward difference** is given by:

$$
\Delta^2 f(x) = \Delta\left[f(x + h) - f(x)\right] = f(x + 2h) - 2f(x + h) + f(x)
$$

---

### Examples:

- $\Delta f(a) = f(a + h) - f(a)$
- $\Delta f(a + h) = f(a + 2h) - f(a + h)$
- $\Delta f(a + 2h) = f(a + 3h) - f(a + 2h)$

---

### In General:

$$
\Delta f(x) = f(x + h) - f(x)
$$

$$
\Delta^2 f(x) = \Delta f(x + h) - \Delta f(x)
$$

Which simplifies to:

$$
\Delta^2 f(x) = f(x + 2h) - 2f(x + h) + f(x)
$$
