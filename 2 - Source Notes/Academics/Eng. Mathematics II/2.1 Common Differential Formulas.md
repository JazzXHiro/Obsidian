# Common Differential Formulas

## 🧮 **Basic Derivatives**

1. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(c) = 0$  
    _Constant rule_
    
2. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(x) = 1$
    
3. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1}$  
    _Power rule_, where $n$ is any real number
    

---

## 🔀 **Derivative of Constant Multiples & Sums**

4. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(cf(x)) = c \cdot f'(x)$  
    _Constant multiple rule_
    
5. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(f(x) + g(x)) = f'(x) + g'(x)$  
    _Sum rule_
    
6. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(f(x) - g(x)) = f'(x) - g'(x)$  
    _Difference rule_
    

---

## ✴️ **Product and Quotient Rules**

7. $\dfrac{d}{dx}[f(x) \cdot g(x)] = f'(x)g(x) + f(x)g'(x)$  
    _Product rule_
    
8. $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left[\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right] = \dfrac{f'(x)g(x) - f(x)g'(x)}{[g(x)]^2}$  
    _Quotient rule_
    

---

## 🔁 **Chain Rule**

9. $\dfrac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$  
    _Chain rule_
    

---

## 🔢 **Trigonometric Functions**

10. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\sin x) = \cos x$
    
11. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\cos x) = -\sin x$
    
12. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\tan x) = \sec^2 x$
    
13. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\cot x) = -\csc^2 x$
    
14. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\sec x) = \sec x \tan x$
    
15. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\csc x) = -\csc x \cot x$
    

---

## 📈 **Inverse Trigonometric Functions**

16. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\sin^{-1}x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}$
    
17. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\cos^{-1}x) = -\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}$
    
18. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\tan^{-1}x) = \dfrac{1}{1 + x^2}$
    
19. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\cot^{-1}x) = -\dfrac{1}{1 + x^2}$
    
20. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\sec^{-1}x) = \dfrac{1}{|x| \sqrt{x^2 - 1}}$
    
21. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\csc^{-1}x) = -\dfrac{1}{|x| \sqrt{x^2 - 1}}$
    

---

## 🧮 **Exponential and Logarithmic Functions**

22. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(e^x) = e^x$
    
23. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(a^x) = a^x \ln a$
    
24. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\ln x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$
    
25. $\dfrac{d}{dx}(\log_a x) = \dfrac{1}{x \ln a}$
