# Algebraic and Transcendental Equation

## 1. Definition

### Algebraic Equation
An equation involving only algebraic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and taking roots) on the variable.

**General Form:**

aₙxⁿ + aₙ₋₁xⁿ⁻¹ + ... + a₁x + a₀ = 0

Where:
- \( a_i \) are constants
- \( x \) is the variable

**Examples:**
- `x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0`
- `x^3 + 2x + 1 = 0`
- `√x - 3 = 0`

---

### Transcendental Equation
An equation that cannot be expressed as a finite polynomial of the variable. Involves **transcendental functions** such as:
- Exponential
- Logarithmic
- Trigonometric
- Inverse Trigonometric

**Examples:**
- `e^x = 3`
- `sin(x) = x/2`
- `x * log(x) = 5`

---

## 2. Differences Between Algebraic and Transcendental Equations

| Feature            | Algebraic Equation         | Transcendental Equation              |
|--------------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Nature             | Polynomial in form         | Involves transcendental functions    |
| Functions involved | Only algebraic             | Exponential, log, trig, etc.         |
| Solvability        | Can often be solved exactly| Require numerical/approx. methods    |
| Example            | `x^2 + 2x + 1 = 0`         | `cos(x) = x`                         |

---

## 3. Solution Methods

### For Algebraic Equations
- **Analytical Methods**
  - Factoring
  - Quadratic formula
  - Synthetic division
- **Numerical Methods**
  - Bisection Method
  - Newton-Raphson Method
  - Regula-Falsi Method

### For Transcendental Equations
- **Numerical Methods Only**
  - Graphical Method
  - Bisection Method
  - Newton-Raphson Method
  - Secant Method

---

## 4. Graphical Understanding

- **Algebraic:** Graphs are smooth curves (parabolas, straight lines).
- **Transcendental:** Graphs may oscillate or grow exponentially (e.g., sine waves, exponential curves).

---

## 5. Applications

| Equation Type      | Applications                              |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Algebraic          | Circuits, control systems, kinematics     |
| Transcendental     | Signal processing, physics, population models |
