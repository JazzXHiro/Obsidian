#### $➋\;$ Solve the following equations by Gauss Elimination Method:

System of equations:  
$$
\begin{aligned}
x - y + 2z &= 3 \\
x + 2y + z &= 5 \\
3x - 4y + 5z &= -13
\end{aligned}
$$

Write in matrix form:  
$$
A \cdot X = B
$$

Where  
$$
A = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1 & 2 \\
1 & 2 & 1 \\
3 & -4 & 5
\end{bmatrix},
\quad
X = \begin{bmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z
\end{bmatrix},
\quad
B = \begin{bmatrix}
3 \\
5 \\
-13
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Augmented matrix:  
$$
[A:B] = 
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1 & 2 & \vert & 3 \\
1 & 2 & 1 & \vert & 5 \\
3 & -4 & 5 & \vert & -13
\end{bmatrix}
$$

---

#### Row operations:

- $R_2 \rightarrow R_2 - R_1$  
- $R_3 \rightarrow R_3 - 3R_1$

New matrix:  
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1 & 2 & \vert & 3 \\
0 & 3 & -1 & \vert & 2 \\
0 & -1 & -1 & \vert & -22
\end{bmatrix}
$$

- $R_3 \rightarrow R_3 + \frac{1}{3}R_2$

Updated matrix:  
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1 & 2 & \vert & 3 \\
0 & 3 & -1 & \vert & 2 \\
0 & 0 & -32 & \vert & -64
\end{bmatrix}
$$

---

#### Back substitution:

From third row:  
$$
-32z = -64 \Rightarrow z = 2
$$

From second row:  
$$
3y - z = 2 \Rightarrow 3y - 2 = 2 \Rightarrow y = 0
$$

From first row:  
$$
x - y + 2z = 3 \Rightarrow x + 0 + 4 = 3 \Rightarrow x = -1
$$

---

#### Final solution:  
$$
x = -1,\quad y = 0,\quad z = 2
$$
