#### **Unicast Transmission**
The process of sending a packet from one host to an Individual host.
![[Pasted image 20250512051119.png||400]]

#### **Broadcast Transmission**
Broadcast Transmission: The process of sending a packet from one host to all the hosts in the network.

##### Limited Broadcast:

> Destination: 255.255.255.255
> 
> Routers do not forward a limited broadcast.

##### Directed Broadcast:
Example-
> Destination: 172.16.4.255
> 
> Hosts within the 172.16.4.0/24 network. (Class C Network)
