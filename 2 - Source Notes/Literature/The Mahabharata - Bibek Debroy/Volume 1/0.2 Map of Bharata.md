## Points of Interest

- **Udaygiri(Odisha)** - 
	  - Udaygiri and Khandagiri Caves on adjacent hills near Bhubaneshwar, Odisha.
	  - 33 Caves in total - 18 on Udaygiri and 15 on Khandagiri hill.
	  - Caves are very Jain in character
	  - Excavated primarily to serve as residential shelters or leṇa for Jain ascetics
