## Points of Interest

- **Udaygiri(Odisha)** - 
	  - Udaygiri and Khandagiri Caves on adjacent hills near Bhubaneshwar, Odisha.
	  - 33 Caves in total - 18 on Udaygiri and 15 on Khandagiri hill.
	  - Caves are very Jain in character
	  - Excavated primarily to serve as residential shelters or leṇa for Jain ascetics.
	  - The name, Udayagiri, which translates from Sanskrit as "Sunrise Hill" or "Sunrise Mountain,". It is a descriptive toponym likely applied to hills facing east, rather than a unique identifier. This underscores a crucial methodological principle in South Asian archaeology: precise identification of a site requires a multi-faceted approach based on geography, chronology, patronage, and cultural affiliation, as a name alone can be misleading.
