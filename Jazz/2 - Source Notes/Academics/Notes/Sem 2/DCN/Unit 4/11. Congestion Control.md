# 11. Congestion Control


### 📌 **1. What is Congestion?**

Congestion occurs in a network when **too many packets** are present in the network, causing **packet loss, delay**, or degraded performance.

It happens when **offered load > network capacity**.

---

### 🎯 **2. Goals of Congestion Control**

- Prevent network collapse
    
- Maintain good throughput and low delay
    
- Ensure fair resource allocation
    
- Avoid retransmissions due to loss
    

---

### 🔄 **3. Congestion vs Flow Control**

|Feature|Congestion Control|Flow Control|
|---|---|---|
|Scope|Network-wide|Between sender and receiver|
|Purpose|Avoid overload in the network|Avoid overwhelming the receiver|
|Example Protocol|TCP Congestion Control|TCP Sliding Window|

---

### ⚙️ **4. Congestion Control Techniques**

#### **A. Open-Loop Control (Prevention)**

- Takes action **before** congestion occurs.
    
- No feedback from the network.
    

|Method|Description|
|---|---|
|Traffic shaping|Controls data entry rate into the network (e.g., Leaky Bucket, Token Bucket)|
|Admission control|Accepts or rejects traffic based on capacity|

---

#### **B. Closed-Loop Control (Reaction)**

- Takes action **after** congestion is detected.
    
- Based on **feedback** from the network.
    

|Method|Description|
|---|---|
|Backpressure|Signal sent to upstream nodes to slow down|
|Choke Packet|Special packet to notify sender to reduce rate|
|Implicit Signaling|Sender infers congestion via delay or loss|
|Explicit Signaling|Router marks packets to inform sender|

---

### 🧠 **5. TCP Congestion Control Mechanisms**

|Mechanism|Description|
|---|---|
|**Slow Start**|Increases congestion window exponentially to probe available bandwidth|
|**Congestion Avoidance**|Increases window linearly to avoid overshooting|
|**Fast Retransmit**|Retransmits lost packet after receiving 3 duplicate ACKs|
|**Fast Recovery**|Reduces congestion window, avoids going back to slow start|

📈 **Congestion Window (cwnd)**: TCP maintains a dynamic limit on the number of unacknowledged packets sent.

---

### 📉 **6. Indicators of Congestion**

- Increased packet delay
    
- Packet loss
    
- Retransmissions
    
- Buffer overflows in routers
    

---

### 🔁 **7. Traffic Shaping Algorithms (Open-Loop)**

|Algorithm|Description|
|---|---|
|**Leaky Bucket**|Transmits at a fixed rate; smooths bursty traffic|
|**Token Bucket**|Allows bursts up to a limit; tokens allow transmission of packets|
