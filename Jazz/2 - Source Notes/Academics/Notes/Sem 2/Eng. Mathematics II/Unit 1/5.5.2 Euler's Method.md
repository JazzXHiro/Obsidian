# Euler's Method

# 📘 Euler & Euler Modified Method

## Euler Method

Euler's iterative formula:

$$
y_{n+1} = y_n + h f(x_n, y_n)
$$

---

### Problem 1:

Solve $\frac{dy}{dx} = x + y$  
Given: $x_0 = 0$, $y_0 = 1$  
Find approximate value of $y$ at $x = 0.1$

---

### Step size:

$$
h = \frac{0.1 - 0}{5} = 0.02
$$

> [!NOTE] Note
> The higher the step-size more accurate the answer is.

### Steps:

- $x_0 = 0$
- $x_1 = 0.02$
- $x_2 = 0.04$
- $x_3 = 0.06$
- $x_4 = 0.08$
- $x_5 = 0.10$

---

### Calculations:

#### Put $n = 0$:
$$
y_1 = y_0 + h (x_0 + y_0) = 1 + 0.02 (0 + 1) = 1.02
$$

#### Put $n = 1$:
$$
y_2 = y_1 + h (x_1 + y_1) = 1.02 + 0.02 (0.02 + 1.02) = 1.0408
$$

#### Put $n = 2$:
$$
y_3 = y_2 + h (x_2 + y_2) = 1.0408 + 0.02 (0.04 + 1.0408) = 1.0624
$$

#### Put $n = 3$:
$$
y_4 = y_3 + h (x_3 + y_3) = 1.0624 + 0.02 (0.06 + 1.0624) = 1.0847
$$

#### Put $n = 4$:
$$
y_5 = y_4 + h (x_4 + y_4) = 1.0847 + 0.02 (0.08 + 1.0847) = 1.108
$$

---

### Final Approximate Answer:
$$
y(0.1) \approx 1.108
$$

# 🧮 Euler's Modified Method  
_(Runge-Kutta Method of Second Order)_

## Formula

$$
y_{n+1}^* = y_n + h f(x_n, y_n)
$$

$$
y_{n+1} = y_n + \frac{h}{2} \left[ f(x_n, y_n) + f(x_{n+1}, y_{n+1}^*) \right]
$$

---

### Given:
Solve $\frac{dy}{dx} = x^2 + y$ with $y(0) = 1$  
Find $y(0.02)$ and $y(0.04)$ using modified Euler's method.

---

### Step size:
$$
h = 0.02
$$

---

### Initial values:
- $x_0 = 0$, $y_0 = 1$
- $x_1 = 0.02$
- $x_2 = 0.04$

---

### First iteration:

$$
f(x_0, y_0) = x_0^2 + y_0 = 0^2 + 1 = 1
$$

#### Intermediate step:

$$
y_1^* = y_0 + h f(x_0, y_0) = 1 + 0.02 \cdot 1 = 1.02
$$

---

### Second iteration:

$$
f(x_1, y_1^*) = x_1^2 + y_1^* = (0.02)^2 + 1.02 = 1.0204
$$

$$
y_1 = y_0 + \frac{h}{2} \left[ f(x_0, y_0) + f(x_1, y_1^*) \right]
$$

$$
= 1 + \frac{0.02}{2} \left( 1 + 1.0204 \right)
= 1 + 0.01 \cdot 2.0204 = 1.020204
$$

---

### Next prediction step:

$$
y_2^* = y_1 + h f(x_1, y_1) = 1.020204 + 0.02 ( (0.02)^2 + 1.020204 ) = 1.040608
$$

---

### Final correction:

$$
f(x_2, y_2^*) = x_2^2 + y_2^* = (0.04)^2 + 1.040608 = 1.042208
$$

$$
y_2 = y_1 + \frac{h}{2} \left[ f(x_1, y_1) + f(x_2, y_2^*) \right]
$$

$$
= 1.020204 + 0.01 (1.020204 + 1.042208) = 1.0408
$$

---

### ✅ Final Results:

- $y(0.02) \approx 1.020204$
- $y(0.04) \approx 1.0408$
