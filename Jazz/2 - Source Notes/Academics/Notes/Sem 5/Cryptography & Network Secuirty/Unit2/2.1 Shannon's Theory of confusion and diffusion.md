# 2.1 Shannon's Theory of confusion and diffusion

Shannon introduced the concept of a **product cipher**, which is a complex cipher combining components like substitution and permutation.

• **Confusion** aims to hide the relationship between the **ciphertext and the key**.

    ◦ The goal is to make the relationship between the key and ciphertext as complex as possible.

    ◦ This is achieved primarily through **non-linearity**.

    ◦ A common element for achieving confusion is **substitution/S-box** (found in both AES and DES).

**Diffusion** aims to hide the relationship between the **ciphertext and the plaintext**.

    ◦ Diffusion spreads out the bits in the message so that any redundancy in the plaintext is scattered over the ciphertext.

    ◦ A simple diffusion element is **bit permutation** (frequently used within DES).

    ◦ Good diffusion requires that each input bit influences most or all output bits, ideally within a few rounds.

• Neither operation alone provides security; the idea is to combine confusion and diffusion elements to build product ciphers.

• Diffusion and confusion are achieved using **iterated product ciphers**, where each iteration is referred to as a **round** and combines components like S-boxes and P-boxes.
