# Adjoint & its properties

#### Adjoint
The **adjoint (or adjugate) of a matrix** is the transpose of its cofactor matrix.

#### Properties
- $A(AdjA) \; = \; AdjA.A = |A|\;I_{n}$
- $|AdjA| = |A|^{n-1}$
- $|A\; AdjA|=|A|^n$
- $adj(adj\;A) = |A|^{n-2}A$
- $(adj\;A)^T=adj(A^T)$
- $Adj\;AB=adjB.adjA$
- $If\;A^T=A \implies (adjA)^T = adjA$
- $|adj(adjA)| = |A|^{(n-1)^2}$
- $adj\;A^n=(adj\;A)^n$
- $adj(bA)=b^{n-1}(adjA)$
