# Properties of Determinants

### **Determinant of a Transpose**

- The determinant of a matrix is equal to the determinant of its transpose: $$\det(A^T) = \det(A)$$
### **Effect of Row and Column Operations**

- **Row Swap:** Swapping two rows (or columns) of a matrix multiplies the determinant by −1.
- **Multiplication by a Scalar:** Multiplying a row (or column) by a scalar $k$ multiplies the determinant by $k$.
- **Row Addition/Subtraction:** Adding or subtracting a multiple of one row (or column) to another does not change the determinant.
