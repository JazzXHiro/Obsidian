# Determinant, Minors & Cofactors

#### **1. Determinant of a Matrix**

The **determinant** of a square matrix is a scalar value that provides important information about the matrix, such as whether it is invertible. The determinant of a matrix $A$ is denoted as $\det(A)$ or $|A|$.

For a **2×2 matrix**:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$$

The determinant is:

$$|A| = ad - bc$$

For a **3×3 matrix**:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b & c \\ d & e & f \\ g & h & i \end{bmatrix}$$

The determinant is computed as:

$$|A| = a \begin{vmatrix} e & f \\ h & i \end{vmatrix} - b \begin{vmatrix} d & f \\ g & i \end{vmatrix} + c \begin{vmatrix} d & e \\ g & h \end{vmatrix}$$

For larger matrices, the determinant is calculated using expansion by **minors and cofactors**

#### Note:

- **Determinant of a upper/lower triangular matrix with prinicipal elements as 1:**
  $$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 100 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1000 & 100 & 1 & 0 \\ 10000 & 1000 & 100 & 1  \end{bmatrix}$$
  Then,
$$\det(A) = 1$$
- **Determinant of a Diagonal Matrix** - product of all the diagonal elements
  $$A =\begin{bmatrix}
10 & 0 & 0&0 \\ 0&11&0&0 \\ 0&0&12&0\\0&0&0&15
\end{bmatrix}$$
$$\det(A)= 10\times 11 \times 12 \times 15$$

#### **2. Minors of a Matrix**

The **minor** of an element $A_{ij}$​ in a matrix is the determinant of the smaller matrix obtained by **removing** the **i-th row** and **j-th column** from the original matrix.

Example:  
For the matrix:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$$

The **minor of element $A_{11}$ (i.e., 1)** is the determinant of:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 5 & 6 \\ 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$$

which is:

$$(5 \times 9) - (6 \times 8) = 45 - 48 = -3$$
#### **3. Cofactors of a Matrix**

The **cofactor** of an element $A_{ij}$​ is given by:

$$C_{ij} = (-1)^{i+j} M_{ij}$$

where:

- $M_{ij}$​ is the **minor** of $A_{ij}$​.
- The sign factor $(-1)^{i+j}$ alternates between positive and negative.

Example:  
For $A_{11}$ in the previous example,

$$C_{11} = (-1)^{1+1} \times (-3) = 1 \times (-3) = -3$$

Similarly, for $A_{12}$​:

$$C_{12} = (-1)^{1+2} \times M_{12} = (-1)^3 \times M_{12} = -M_{12}$$
