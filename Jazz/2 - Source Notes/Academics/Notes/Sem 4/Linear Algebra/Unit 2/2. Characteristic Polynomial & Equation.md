# Characteristic Polynomial & Equation

### **Characteristic Polynomial**
For a square matrix $A$ of size $n \times n$, the **characteristic polynomial** is defined as:

$$p(\lambda) = \det(A - \lambda I)$$

where:
- $A$ is the given square matrix,
- $\lambda$ is a scalar (representing an eigenvalue),
- $I$ is the identity matrix of the same size as $A$,
- $\det$ denotes the determinant of a matrix.

The characteristic polynomial is a polynomial of degree $n$ in the variable $\lambda$.

---

### **Characteristic Equation**
The **characteristic equation** is obtained by setting the characteristic polynomial equal to zero:

$$p(\lambda) = 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \det(A - \lambda I) = 0$$

This equation is used to find the **eigenvalues** of the matrix $A$. The roots of the characteristic equation (i.e., the solutions for $\lambda$ ) are the eigenvalues of $A$.

---

### **Steps to Find the Characteristic Polynomial and Equation**
1. **Subtract $\lambda I$ from $A$:**
   $$A - \lambda I$$
   This results in a matrix where $\lambda$ is subtracted from each diagonal entry of $A$.

2. **Compute the determinant:**
   $$\det(A - \lambda I)$$
   This gives the characteristic polynomial $p(\lambda)$.

3. **Set the determinant to zero:**
   $$\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$$
   This is the characteristic equation.

4. **Solve for $\lambda$:**
   The solutions to this equation are the eigenvalues of $A$.

---

### **Example**
Consider a $2 \times 2$ matrix:

$$A = \begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{pmatrix}$$

1. Subtract $\lambda I$:
   $$A - \lambda I = \begin{pmatrix}
   a - \lambda & b \\
   c & d - \lambda
   \end{pmatrix}$$

2. Compute the determinant:
   $$\det(A - \lambda I) = (a - \lambda)(d - \lambda) - bc$$
   This simplifies to:
   $$p(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - (a + d)\lambda + (ad - bc)$$

3. Set the determinant to zero:
   $$\lambda^2 - (a + d)\lambda + (ad - bc) = 0$$
   This is the characteristic equation.

4. Solve for $\lambda$ to find the eigenvalues.

---
#### **Short Trick For 2x2 & 3x3 Matrix**

**For 2x2 Matrix**:

$$λ² - \text{(Trace of A)}λ + det A = 0$$

**For Characteristic Equation**

For 3x3 Matrix

$$λ³ - \text{(Trace of A)}λ² + \text{(Minor Along diagonal)}λ - det A = 0$$

### **Key Points**
- The characteristic polynomial is always of degree $n$ for an $n \times n$ matrix.
- The eigenvalues of $A$ are the roots of the characteristic equation.
- The characteristic polynomial and equation are fundamental tools for analyzing the properties of matrices, such as diagonalization and stability in systems of differential equations.
