# SDLC
![[Pasted image 20250220061806.png]]

The term "**Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)**" traditionally focuses on phases up to deployment (requirements, design, implementation, testing, deployment). The description here includes **operation, maintenance, and retirement**, which technically fall under the broader **Software Life Cycle (SLC)**. However, in modern contexts, many use "SDLC" to encompass the entire lifespan of the software, including retirement.
