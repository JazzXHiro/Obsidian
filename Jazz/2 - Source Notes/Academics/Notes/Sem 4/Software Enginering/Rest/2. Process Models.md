# Process Models
**Process models** in software engineering are structured frameworks that define the steps, activities, and workflows for developing software systems. They provide a systematic approach to guide teams through the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), ensuring consistency, quality, and efficiency. Different models cater to varying project needs, risks, and complexities.
