# 1. Software Quality

# Software Quality

- **Definition**: The extent to which software meets **requirements** and **user expectations**.  
- **Key Aspects**:  
  - Functionality  
  - Reliability  
  - Usability  
  - Efficiency  
  - Maintainability  
  - Portability  

- **Misconception**:  
  A bug-free product ≠ high quality (it’s just one attribute).
