# Setting/Premise

- Story inspired by the isladen valley woman murder
- Location is inspired by the fears to fathom iron-bark lookout game which is inspired by the rainier national park, Washington, USA

- Place - A forest Trail in Northern parts of the USA
- Time - At night (from 8:00 pm to 4:00 am) on 29 November 1990
- Landmarks - 
	- A bus stop at the foothills
	- A cabin where the trail starts
	- A Three way point (Three routes - To the cabin, To the lookout, An abandoned one which leads to the mines)
	- A Cave/(mine?)
	- A high point (Maybe with a lookout tower)

# Characters

1. The main character - journalist who is sent to document the scene for the a major newspaper.

2. The pickup truck dude who hitchhiked our mc to the bus stop for the trail and he can pick us up at 4:00 in the morning as soon as his work is done - ==This creates a sense of urgency and a ticking clock effect for the game==
	- Gives the context about the area and warns about occult and recent mis-happening in the areas.
	- Set ups the vibe of the game.

3. Suspect of being murder victims - (creates uncertainty)

	- A well off woman who came to trail for unknown reason.
		- AGE - early 30s
		- Reason for visit - unknown
		- Reason for murder - unknown (==adds to the mystery of the place==)
		
	- A old man who is in a massive debt
		- AGE - around late 60s
		- Reason for visit - unknown (maybe running)
		- suspected Reason for murder - Killed by the loan sharks
		
	- A young teenage boy who has been bullying by a bunch of punks
		- AGE - 16
		- Reason for visit - A local boy who hikes often
		- suspected Reason for murder - Beef with the punks
		  
	1. Two of these victims are distractions (Old man and the boy) to not make the game linear and predictable. And the lady is the real victim.
	2. Lady backstory - She came to nearby town for unknown reasons, then went on the trail. did not return and was found dead (==this is not revealed until the end of the game==).
	3. The old man also came a couple days ago to the town and also went missing the same day.
	4. The boy went for the usual evening hike and did not return.
	5. The boy and the old man are later discovered by the recuse team the next day.
# Lore

1. The trail has been there for a long time but a decade ago it saw an emergence of cultist going there to practice the occult.
2. The occult practices were then banned and restricted but the locals still believe that it has not fully vanished.
3. Also due to recent murder that happened, the suspicion has only grown stronger.
4. The local people's opinion has been divided - Some think that it's the work of the cultist and some say that it was a murder.
5. The trail is not particularly famous but people come for site seeing and it opens from 10:00 am to 6:00 pm.

# Clues Explained - 

### Lady Clues -

1. Suitcase 
	1. Type: Intractable
	2. States - locked
	3. Access thru password (3 digit) - the worn out digits on the dial gives the hint
	4. Location - Inside cabin
	5. Flavour - Clothes, A train ticket (boarding date gone) and a hotel key
		1. Train ticket: Hints that the lady never left the town 
			- "Single ticket: Ashford (nearest town) to Olympia (Capital city of Washington) dated 27/11/1990"
			- Deduction: She never left
		2. Hotel key: Hints she is well off
			1. "Grand Hotel, the kind of place only the high status people can afford"
			2. Deduction: She is rich

2. Group of Bottles with an oily smell
	1. Type: World Item
		1. Location On the way to the mine.
		2. "These Bottles smell faintly of benzine, someone brought fuel here, this might be the same thing used for the act of murder"
		3. Deduction: These bottles are connected to the case
			
3. Broken Wrist watch (5 pieces)
	1. Type: Scattered around the map (clue not revealed until all pieces are found)
	2. Location: Last one is at the mine entrance and its burnt.
	3. Detection type: makes ticking noises as the player gets in the range of the collectible and it gets louder and fainter.
	4. "The Watch is a really expensive one, The watch is stopped at 2:13 — the moment everything stopped"
	5. Deduction: The lady is the victim

### Other clues ( To throw the player off )-

1. Clue #1 - Letter outside safe house on the mail box (Old man)
	1. Type: Intractable
	2. The contents are displayed on the screen and Disappears after interaction
	3. location: near safe house
	4. "Dad left home before dawn. He said he’d fix it all himself this time. I think he owed money to someone dangerous"
	5. Deduction: He might be the victim

2. Clue #2 - Threat note (Boy)
	1. Type: Intractable
	2. The contents are displayed on the screen and Disappears after interaction
	3. location: near bus stop
	4. "How dare you say those things! You think you’re untouchable? I’ll make you pay for it, bastard!"
	5. Deduction: He might be the victim

3. Clue #3 - A climbing stick (Old man)
	1. Type: World Item
	2. location: near the look out
	3. "A splintered climbing stick. One end is cleanly cut, the other chipped from a fall. Someone struggled to stay upright here"
	4. Deduction: He lost his stick which suggests he might be in trouble

4. Clue #4 - Half burnt pocket knife (Boy)
	1. Type: World Item
	2. location: around the site but not exactly on it
	3. "A small pocket knife, handle charred. The blade’s edge is darkened, was it burned?"
	4. deduction: He was involved with fire somehow
	5. Later a extinguished campfire near the outlook is discovered and the rest of the camp tool kit for young boys too
