# Chłopcy

The Polish word „chłopcy” (meaning "boys", plural of chłopiec) is pronounced:

IPA: /ˈxwɔp.t͡sɨ/

Breakdown:

ch → pronounced like a harsh h, similar to the ch in loch or the j in Spanish jalapeño (IPA: /x/)

ł → pronounced like English w

o → like English o in lot

p → like English p

c → pronounced ts (like in cats)

y → like a short i in myth, or a Russian ы


Approximate English pronunciation:

"HWOHP-tsih" (say it quickly and smoothly)
