# Lubicie

The Polish word „lubicie” (meaning “you [plural] like”) is pronounced:

IPA: /luˈbi.t͡ɕɛ/

Breakdown:

lu → like loo in loop

bi → like bee

cie → pronounced as "će", similar to "cheh", but with a very soft ch/tch sound (like in cheese, but palatalized)


Approximate English pronunciation:

loo-BEE-cheh
(With the “cheh” very soft and delicate — not a hard "ch" like in chair)
