# Wy

The Polish word „wy” (meaning “you” [plural/informal group]) is pronounced:

IPA: /vɨ/

Breakdown:

w → pronounced like English v

y → a unique Polish vowel, kind of like the i in myth, but a bit deeper and further back in the mouth (not like English ee)


Approximate English pronunciation:

"vih" (but not exactly like vie or vee — more like vih, with the tongue further back)

Tips:

Avoid saying "wee" or "why"

Focus on keeping the y sound short and unrounded
