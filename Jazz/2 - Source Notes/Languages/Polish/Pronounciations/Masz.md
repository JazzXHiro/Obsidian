# Masz

The Polish word „masz” (meaning "you have" in informal singular) is pronounced:

IPA: /maʂ/

Breakdown:

m → like English m

a → like a in father

sz → pronounced like sh in shoe (IPA: /ʂ/)


Approximate English pronunciation:

“Mahsh” (rhymes with gosh)
