# Przepraszam

The Polish word „przepraszam” means “I’m sorry” or “Excuse me” — used both to apologize and to politely get someone’s attention.


---

✅ Pronunciation:

IPA: /pʂɛˈpra.ʂam/


---

Breakdown:

prze → pronounced "pshe"

"prz" makes a sound like “psh” in push, but stronger

"e" like e in bed


pra → like "prah" (as in prada)

szam → like "sham" (but the sh is a bit softer and broader)



---

Approximate English pronunciation:

“psheh-PRAH-sham”

✅ Tip: Say it smoothly, with stress on "PRAH"
It should flow like: psheh-PRAH-sham
