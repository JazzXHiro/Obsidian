# Oni

The Polish word „oni” (meaning “they” — masculine or mixed gender) is pronounced:

IPA: /ˈɔ.ɲi/

Breakdown:

o → like o in not (rounded, short)

n + i → together make a soft "ń" sound, like the "ny" in canyon

i → like ee in see


Approximate English pronunciation:

“OH-ny” (with the "ny" part soft like in onion)
