# Xenoblade Chronicles 2

#### Score - 9.64/10

##### Things I Didn't Like -

1. The blade skill tree.
2. The field skill system.
3. The combat system is not that good but better than XC 1.
4. The dialogues were awkward at times.
5. The delivery of the dialogues were off at times --- Rex can't scream ffs.
6. Not being able to attack while moving.
7. Tutorials do suck
8. The map system was horrible
9. The UI is not that good at times
10. Most of the side quests were forgettable
11. Poppy well while being a cool character --- The way to utilizing poppy's full potential is extremely tedious and repetitive and well very complex.
12. Sexualization on Poppy.
#### Things I Loved -
13. The Setting is absolute genius --- Continents that are alive (The Titans)
14. Blades have a persona, they can communicate and share emotions the same as you
15. The bond that is developed between a blade and a driver is one of the core things that kept me invested in the story.
16. The pace of world building is phenomenal, the way this game presents its world is absolutely beautiful.
17. Loved the Memory scenes.
18. The game doesn't ignore it's characters, utilized and builds upon all of them.
19. Loved the aspect Pyra/Mythra being two personalities in a single vessel.
20. Loved the rest and level up system.
21. Music is peak
22. I love the characters and the idea of them being in a party that actually feels like a family on an adventure.
23. I love small intricate details to the plotline.
24. Xenoblade Chronicles 2 was more relatable than 1.
25. Jin..... 
26. I personally liked Rex more than Shulk.

	### **"Xenoblade 2 is about losing hope, what that does to a person, how it damages their will to live; yet, it's also about rediscovering that hope; that anyone, no matter how far gone, can find it again. We have to find it again. There is no other way forward."**
